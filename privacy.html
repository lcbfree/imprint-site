Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026. Applies to Imprint for iPhone, version 1.1 and later.

Imprint is made by Changbo Liu, an independent developer. The short version: your photos are processed on your iPhone and never leave it. I don't run a server, I don't collect any data, and I can't see your photos, locations or purchases.

Photos

You choose photos with the system photo picker. Imprint reads them on your device to find each photo's capture date, draw the date stamp and crop it to your print size. The finished photos are saved as new files in your photo library. Your original photos are not changed or deleted.

Imprint asks for permission to add photos to your library, and for full photo access only so it can find or create the album you name. Without full access it still works and saves to Recents instead.

Finished photos keep the capture date. They do not include GPS coordinates.

Location and place names

Adding place names is off by default. If you turn on Place, Imprint reads the coordinates stored in your photos and sends them to Apple's geocoding service (part of iOS) to get a place name such as a city or park. This is the only time Imprint uses the network. Apple handles these requests under the Apple Privacy Policy; I don't receive the coordinates or the results.

Each looked-up place name is stored on your iPhone together with its coordinates for up to 30 days, so the same place isn't looked up twice. This cache stays on the device and is excluded from backups. Imprint does not ask for access to your current location.

Purchases

Imprint Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; I only receive Apple's standard sales reports, which do not identify you. To keep the free allowance consistent, Imprint stores the number of photos you've saved in your iPhone's keychain, which can sync through iCloud Keychain. This number stays within Apple's services and I can't access it.

What Imprint does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No uploading of photos to any server.

Children

Imprint does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version changes what is described here, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.
