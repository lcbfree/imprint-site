Every photo dated the day it was taken, ready to print.

Imprint stamps each photo with its own capture date, crops it to your print size, and saves the whole batch to one album.

  1. Pick photosChoose a batch from your library, from any years.
  2. Check the previewEvery photo already looks like the print: cropped to 4×6 or the size you chose, stamped in one of 12 styles. Tap one to adjust it.
  3. Save to an albumFinished copies go into an album you name. Your originals stay unchanged.

Everything happens on your iPhone. No account, no uploads, no ads. Your first 20 photos are free; Imprint Pro is a one-time purchase for unlimited photos.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.

  3. 保存到相册成品存进你命名的相册，原图不会改动。
