Common questions

Where are my finished photos?

In the Photos app, in the album you named when saving. If Imprint didn't have full photo access, they're in Recents instead. The completion screen tells you which.

Some photos say they have no date.

Those photos don't contain a capture date, which is common for screenshots and images saved from chat apps. Imprint never guesses a date. Tap the photo to enter one, leave it without a date, or remove it from the batch.

How do I add place names?

Turn on Place in the preview. It's off by default. Photos without location data won't show a place, and you can type or change a place name on any photo.

How do I restore Imprint Pro on a new iPhone?

In Imprint, tap the gear icon on the home screen, tap Unlock Imprint Pro, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How does the free allowance work?

Your first 20 saved photos are free, with every feature included. The count doesn't reset when you reinstall. After that, Imprint Pro is a one-time purchase for unlimited photos.

How do I print them?

Upload the album to any print service that accepts photos from your library. Files are named by date and number, so sorting by name keeps them in the order they were taken.

常见问题

保存好的照片在哪里？

在"照片"App 里你保存时命名的相册中。如果印相没有完整照片权限，照片会在"最近项目"里。完成页会写明存到了哪里。

有些照片提示没有日期。

这些照片本身没有记录拍摄日期，截图和从聊天软件保存的图片常见这种情况。印相不会猜日期。点这张照片，可以手动输入日期、不标日期，或从这批移出。

怎么加上地名？

在预览页打开"地点"，默认是关闭的。没有位置信息的照片不会显示地点；任何一张都可以手动输入或修改地名。

换了新 iPhone，怎么恢复 Imprint Pro？

在印相首页点右上角的齿轮进入设置，点"解锁 Imprint Pro"，再点"恢复购买"。需要登录购买时使用的同一个 Apple 账户。

免费额度怎么算？

前 20 张成功保存的照片免费，所有功能都能用，卸载重装不会清零。之后一次性购买 Imprint Pro，不限张数。

怎么拿去冲印？

把相册上传到任何支持从图库选图的冲印服务即可。文件按日期和序号命名，按文件名排序就是拍摄顺序。
